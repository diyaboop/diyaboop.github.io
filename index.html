CIFAR-10 Robustness & Fairness Audit
What Aggregate Accuracy Hides

End-to-end responsible AI audit pipeline applied to a CIFAR-10 image classifier — surfacing systematic class bias, robustness failures under real-world conditions, and confidence calibration issues that aggregate accuracy metrics hide. Three audit modules: bias audit, robustness audit, and calibration audit.
